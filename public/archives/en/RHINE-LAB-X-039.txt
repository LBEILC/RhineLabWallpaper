GALLERIA STELLARIA

Department: Observatory Records Team
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Kristen Wright
Access: REFERENCE AREA

Galleria Stellaria is a stargazing space designed by Kristen. Its central planetarium presents celestial clues that invite further questions. It is both a facility and a reflection of its designer's personal pursuit. This volume examines how a space can carry a researcher's imagination, and how imagination can approach verifiable observation.

Research notes
1. Record what the planetarium depicts separately from data obtained through observation.
2. Read the facility's arrangement alongside its designer's research goals.
3. A private stargazing space may connect to plans far beyond the scale of one individual.
