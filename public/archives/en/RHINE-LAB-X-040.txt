HALL OF STASIS

Department: Remains Investigation Team
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Investigation Team
Access: REFERENCE AREA

The facilities and sealed devices left in the Hall of Stasis confront investigators with many unexplained phenomena. Incomplete information is itself a defining feature of the site. This volume preserves observations, remains, and unresolved questions, avoiding a complete history assembled from insufficient evidence.

Research notes
1. Record observations separately from speculation about a device's purpose.
2. Preserve descriptions of the original state of objects that cannot be opened or explained.
3. Later evidence may supplement earlier records, but should not erase their original questions.
