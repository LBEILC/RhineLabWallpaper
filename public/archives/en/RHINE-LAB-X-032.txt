FOCUS GENERATOR

Department: Joint Project Team
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Kristen Wright
Access: REFERENCE AREA

The focus generator is a key facility connecting large-scale engineering with the high-altitude plan in Lone Trail. It concentrates energy obtained over long distances for a particular purpose, binding facility design to project intent. This volume uses the device to examine the distance between public objectives and what participants actually know.

Research notes
1. Catalog a device's function separately from the project's public account of it.
2. Track which people control and operate critical stages.
3. When examining one device, retain the context of the larger engineering project to which it belongs.
