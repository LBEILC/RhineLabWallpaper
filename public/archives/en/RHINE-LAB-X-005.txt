ECOLOGICAL OBSERVATION

Department: Ecological Section
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Muelsyse
Access: REFERENCE AREA

Led by Muelsyse, the Ecological Section studies relationships between life and the environment and conducts joint research with Rhodes Island. Clean experimental conditions have particular significance for her. Beginning with continuous observations of water and plants, this volume asks how much laboratory stability can explain changes in the world outside.

Research notes
1. Compare water, plant, and environmental records without treating correlation as proof of causation.
2. Record sampling sites and conditions, including differences that cannot be reproduced in the laboratory.
3. Environmental purification affects both research results and the researcher's own needs.
