A SHIFTING LABORATORY

Department: Engineering Section
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Trimounts Laboratory
Access: REFERENCE AREA

The laboratories in Lone Trail feature gravitational conditions that affect movement, with mechanisms and movable objects changing how space can be used. This volume studies such facilities: when the floor is no longer a stable reference, routes, anchoring methods, and operating habits all need reconsideration.

Research notes
1. Record safe positions for people and equipment before and after environmental changes.
2. Include the paths of moving objects in access and observation plans.
3. Make the conditions for using the same space under different states clearly distinguishable.
