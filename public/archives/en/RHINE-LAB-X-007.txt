INSTRUMENT CALIBRATION

Department: Engineering Section
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Mayer
Access: REFERENCE AREA

Mayer prepared and calibrated experimental apparatus for a project involving Silence, while continuing her mechanical designs at the Lutra Workshop. Instruments turn researchers' ideas into measurable signals; errors introduced at this stage can be amplified by every calculation that follows.

Research notes
1. Preserve the relationship between measurement standards, equipment condition, and raw readings.
2. Equipment handovers must explain operating conditions and maintenance procedures.
3. The practical knowledge held by calibration staff should form part of the project record.
