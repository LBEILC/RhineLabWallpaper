RHINE LAB, LLC.

Department: Control Section
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Kristen Wright / Saria
Access: REFERENCE AREA

Rhine Lab grew from a laboratory co-founded by Kristen and Saria. It brings together researchers studying life, energy, and unknown technologies, while facing pressure from funding, power, and the uses of its discoveries. Understanding the institution means reading both its experimental records and the choices made by its researchers.

Research notes
1. Research spans several sections, and major projects often depend on collaboration across them.
2. The technical value of a discovery and the way it is ultimately used must be considered separately.
3. Cross-reference institutional history with personal records to preserve changes in perspective over time.
