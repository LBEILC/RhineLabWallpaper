POINTS OF CONTACT

Department: Cooperation Affairs Team
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Dorothy / Rhodes Island
Access: REFERENCE AREA

Dorothy joined the collaboration as the person responsible for a joint project between Rhine Lab and Rhodes Island. Institutional relationships thus took shape through specific people, records, and research tasks. This volume considers how cooperation builds trust and how clearly bounded collaboration can continue when the parties hold different positions.

Research notes
1. State the scope of joint research and each party's responsibilities clearly.
2. Assigned personnel provide a channel for communication without implying complete institutional agreement.
3. Cooperation should allow questions to be raised, not only showcase achievements already agreed upon.
