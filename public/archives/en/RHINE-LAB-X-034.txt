RESONANCE DEVICES

Department: Originium Arts Section
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Dorothy Franks
Access: REFERENCE AREA

Dorothy specializes in the application of Originium Arts, and her resonance devices offer one route into understanding her research. She hopes to improve others' circumstances, but good intentions do not by themselves establish whether a method is appropriate. This volume places technical performance beside research motives and preserves the questions between them.

Research notes
1. Record observable effects separately from the researcher's hopes for a device.
2. An overall goal must not eclipse participants' experiences and choices.
3. Label unknown mechanisms clearly rather than presenting wishes as verified results.
