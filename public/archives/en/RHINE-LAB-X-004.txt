Ferdinand is closely associated with Rhine Lab's Energy Section and the resources invested in major projects. Large energy programs are therefore both scientific work and organizational collaboration. This volume explores the relationship between efficiency, supply, and outside interests as energy research moves into practical use.

Research notes
1. Record connections between energy sources, conversion stages, and final uses.
2. As scale increases, one successful experiment cannot replace validation of sustained operation.
3. A project's funding arrangements can also change its technical direction and the uses of its results.
