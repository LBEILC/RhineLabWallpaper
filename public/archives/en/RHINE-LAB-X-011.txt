SIGNAL & NOISE

Department: Data & Records Team
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Ptilopsis
Access: REFERENCE AREA

Ptilopsis once maintained data at Rhine Lab and continues to assist with medical records and information at Rhodes Island. Searchable data is not automatically reliable knowledge. This volume examines the boundaries between recording, filtering, and interpretation, particularly the possibility that unusual signals may be discarded as noise.

Research notes
1. Keep both raw records and processed results so filtering criteria can be reconsidered.
2. Mark missing and anomalous data explicitly instead of filling it in by default.
3. Interpret search results in the context in which their underlying records were created.
