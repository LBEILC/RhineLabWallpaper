LIFE ON THE ICEFIELD

Department: Scientific Investigation Section
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Magallan
Access: REFERENCE AREA

Magallan's icefield expeditions continually bring distant observations back to research institutions. In places rarely visited, specimens, terrain, and environmental conditions must be recorded together. This volume considers how to preserve field details when information is limited, so later studies can return to the place and circumstances of the original observation.

Research notes
1. Catalog specimens together with the environments in which they were collected.
2. Preserve images, routes, and descriptions for observations that cannot be repeated.
3. Remain open to unknown phenomena while stating the limits of the supporting evidence.
