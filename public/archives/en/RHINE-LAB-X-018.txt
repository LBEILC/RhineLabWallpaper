SCIENCE & ITS PEOPLE

Department: Control Section
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Silence
Access: REFERENCE AREA

Silence initiated a joint declaration on scientific ethics in Trimounts and became involved in related ethical affairs. She brought questions encountered in laboratories and clinical care into a wider discussion. This volume explores a persistent challenge: how individual people can retain a voice as science advances.

Research notes
1. Discuss the circumstances of research subjects, not only the benefits research might bring.
2. Record consent, dissent, and withdrawal as ongoing processes.
3. Test principles repeatedly against specific cases instead of treating written rules as the end of inquiry.
