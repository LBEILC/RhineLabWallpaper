OBSERVER’S JOURNAL

Department: Scientific Investigation Section
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Magallan
Access: REFERENCE AREA

Magallan spends extended periods exploring remote regions, returning repeatedly to the icefields. A field trip may yield only a short recording or a single specimen, yet still remove a blind spot for the next researcher. This volume examines how field records gradually become knowledge that others can use.

Research notes
1. Preserve routes, weather, and observation positions to explain the conditions in which evidence was gathered.
2. List unexplained phenomena as questions for comparison with later observations.
3. Archives should preserve wrong turns and unfinished surveys as well as successful discoveries.
