THE DIABOLIC INCIDENT

Department: Historical Events Team
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Ifrit / Silence / Saria
Access: REFERENCE AREA

The Diabolic Incident changed the relationships between Ifrit, Silence, and Saria and exposed profound problems in Rhine Lab's research. Focusing on its consequences, this volume preserves threads of care, misunderstanding, and responsibility. The later lives of those affected are also part of the incident's record.

Research notes
1. Cross-reference experimental records with subsequent experiences of care.
2. Identify gaps in the evidence instead of filling every blank with one explanation.
3. Consequences include changes in relationships and lives as well as damage to facilities.
