CALCIFICATION STUDIES

Department: Originium Arts Records Team
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Saria
Access: REFERENCE AREA

Saria's Arts involve calcium and its compounds and work in conjunction with her medical knowledge. Protection, support, and environmental effects all appear in this ability. This volume treats it as an applied research case, examining how purpose determines the use of the same technique.

Research notes
1. Connect the expression of an ability with its purpose, recipients, and range.
2. Compare the precision required for protection and medical support.
3. Avoid measuring the value of Originium Arts solely by destructive power.
