MEEBOO WORKBENCH

Department: Lutra Workshop
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Mayer
Access: REFERENCE AREA

Meeboos are machines designed and operated by Mayer, and creations to which she is deeply attached. Maintenance, naming, and the preservation of old parts gradually narrow the distance between tool and companion. From the workbench, this volume examines how a creator understands the machines she has made.

Research notes
1. Record the parts and lessons retained through successive designs.
2. Include the operator's familiarity among the conditions for using equipment.
3. A maintenance record also tells the story of a creation gradually taking shape.
