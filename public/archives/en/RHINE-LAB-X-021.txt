R-series powered armor appears in experimental facilities associated with Rhine Lab, bringing engineering equipment into direct contact with armed applications. Beginning with maintenance and operating conditions, this volume examines how complex devices depend on cooperation and how research gains new identities once it leaves the workbench.

Research notes

2. Observe operators, maintenance staff, and machinery as a connected system.
3. Record changes of purpose instead of explaining every later use through the original research goal.
