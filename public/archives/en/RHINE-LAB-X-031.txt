DISTANT TRANSMISSION

Department: Energy Section
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Trimounts Project
Access: REFERENCE AREA

Lone Trail's focus generators are connected to energy transmission from the ground. As distance increases, transmitters, receivers, and coordination together determine system performance. This volume examines the interdependence of each link and treats interruptions, deviations, and recovery as states equally deserving of observation.

Research notes
1. Cross-check transmission, reception, and control signals on the same timeline.
2. When a link fails, distinguish equipment problems from changes in external conditions.
3. Do not judge the entire transmission process only by the energy that arrives successfully.
