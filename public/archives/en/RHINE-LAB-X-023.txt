IFRIT: RECOVERY

Department: Medical Cooperation Team

Ifrit was once a patient at Rhine Lab and later received care from Silence at Rhodes Island. Her story should not end with unusual powers or experimental experiences. This volume centers recovery, learning, and everyday relationships as she gradually understands her abilities and builds a life of her own.

Research notes
1. Record physical condition alongside everyday adjustment instead of tracking abilities alone.
2. Preserve her own words rather than allowing an observer's account to replace her voice.
3. Distinguish the effects of past events from changes taking place now.
