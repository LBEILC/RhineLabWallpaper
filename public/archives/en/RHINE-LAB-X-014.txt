Rhine Lab's work takes place across interdependent sections responsible for research, equipment, personnel, and defense. Silence's later changes in role also connect scientific decisions with organizational responsibility. Following collaboration between sections, this volume traces how information moves through the institution and where it can be interrupted.

Research notes
1. Distinguish formal duties from temporary working relationships formed during major projects.
2. Handover records should describe unresolved questions as well as completed work.
3. When a project crosses section boundaries, clarify who can raise objections and ask further questions.
