PATRONS & RESEARCH

Department: Institutional History Team
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Maylander Foundation
Access: REFERENCE AREA

Rhine Lab's development is intertwined with outside supporters. Investigative records connect the institution's early growth with organizations such as the Maylander Foundation. This volume considers how support makes research possible and how accepting it can change research goals and the freedom to make decisions.

Research notes
1. Map financial, equipment, and personnel support within the same network of project relationships.
2. Record possible tensions between the terms of cooperation and research objectives.
3. Discuss the intended uses of results before those results are formally delivered.
