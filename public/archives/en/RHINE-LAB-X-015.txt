LIFE IN TRIMOUNTS

Department: Regional Records Team
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Muelsyse
Access: REFERENCE AREA

Trimounts is more than the setting of scientific events: it is a city where researchers work, socialize, and live. Muelsyse's records reveal interests and experiences beyond the laboratory. This volume approaches a scientific city through everyday moments, remembering that grand research programs are always carried out by individual people.

Research notes
1. Include living spaces as well as research spaces in a person's background.
2. People in different circumstances have different routes and memories within the same city.
3. Preserve everyday details so that a person is not reduced to a position and a list of achievements.
