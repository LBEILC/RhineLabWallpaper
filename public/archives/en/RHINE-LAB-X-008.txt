FILE X-008 / Beyond the Horizon
BEYOND THE HORIZON

Department: Control Section
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Kristen Wright
Access: REFERENCE AREA

Kristen directed her research beyond Terra's sky. In Lone Trail, that pursuit turned questions about the heavens into an event involving all of Trimounts. This volume collects observations on that theme while preserving the unresolved tensions between ambition, secrecy, and cost.

Research notes
1. Examine the pursuit of the unknown separately from the methods used to carry out a specific project.
2. Compare participants' judgments of the same event in light of the information available to them.
3. After a boundary is crossed, those left behind must still face the consequences on the ground.
