Public investigations into Rhine Lab trace its early laboratory to the partnership between Kristen and Saria. The founders shared ambitions but gradually came to different judgments. This volume places the institution's beginnings beside its later choices to examine how shared ideals withstand practical pressures.

Research notes
1. Trace development through both the early laboratory and the incorporated organization.
2. Distinguish retrospective accounts from information available at the time.
3. Founding an institution together does not imply agreement on every subsequent study.
