MATERIAL MEMORY

Department: Engineering Section
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Engineering Section
Access: REFERENCE AREA

Powered armor fits materials, power, and control systems into a limited space. Rhine Lab's R-series equipment offers a way to examine these constraints. This volume studies material changes under repeated stress, asking whether a device that seems to have returned to normal still carries traces of its last use inside.

Research notes
1. Record external appearance and internal condition separately rather than relying on visual inspection alone.
2. Compare the maintenance needs of replaceable parts and primary load-bearing components.
3. Include fatigue, wear, and repeated assembly in the same equipment history.
