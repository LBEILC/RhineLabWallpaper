STRUCTURE & BOUNDARIES

Department: Engineering Section
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Engineering Section
Access: REFERENCE AREA

Turning a laboratory idea into a working facility requires practical answers about loads, maintenance, and access. Rhine Lab's engineers provide equipment for major research projects. This volume examines the boundaries of facilities: who maintains an idea once it is built, and who bears the consequences when it fails.

Research notes
1. Load-bearing structures and the Structural Section's life-science work concern different kinds of structure.
2. Compare the maintenance space needed by experimental apparatus and long-term facilities.
3. Provide access routes for unexpected shutdowns; records must cover more than normal operation.
