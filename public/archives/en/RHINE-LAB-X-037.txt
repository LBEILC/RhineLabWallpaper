LONE TRAIL

Department: High-Altitude Research Records Team
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Kristen Wright
Access: REFERENCE AREA

Lone Trail takes Kristen's exploration beyond the sky and prompts those on the ground to reconsider their relationship with Rhine Lab. This volume follows the path of that event: every advance preserves some connections while also bringing farewells that cannot be undone.

Research notes
1. Compare actions in the sky with responses on the ground in chronological order.
2. Preserve different characters' understandings of the same moment.
3. Exploration matters both for where it reaches and for the questions it allows later generations to ask.
