GREENHOUSE & HOME

Department: Ecological Section
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Muelsyse
Access: REFERENCE AREA

Muelsyse's identity as an elf closely connects her environmental research with her own circumstances. The greenhouse is both a research site and an expression of the search for a livable space. This volume considers preservation and change: how can an environment that can be maintained become a place where life can truly be lived?

Research notes
1. Observe environmental needs alongside personal experience.
2. Distinguish preserving a specimen from sustaining a way of life.
3. Research can explain environmental change, but it cannot decide someone's sense of belonging for them.
