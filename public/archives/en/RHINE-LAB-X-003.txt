THE CONTOUR OF LIFE

Department: Structural Section
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Silence
Access: REFERENCE AREA

At Rhine Lab, Silence conducted life-science research involving Originium before becoming a medical operator at Rhodes Island. Her experience makes the distance between a research subject and a patient tangible: a seemingly complete experiment may still leave unanswered how its subjects will live afterward.

Research notes
1. Observe changes in tissue, function, and personal experience within the same framework.
2. Cross-check clinical care records against the goals and assumptions of the experimental phase.
3. Preserve uncertainties in research instead of hiding them behind polished conclusions.
