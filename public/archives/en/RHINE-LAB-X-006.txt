LABORATORY SAFETY

Department: Defense Section
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Saria
Access: REFERENCE AREA

Saria combines a scientific background with experience in handling exceptional incidents and once headed Rhine Lab's Defense Section. Her history suggests that laboratory safety extends beyond access controls and equipment. When project goals conflict with individual lives, someone must be able to stop the research and protect those present.

Research notes
1. Preparation should include evacuation routes, contact responsibilities, and stop conditions.
2. Incident reports must identify who was affected, as well as which equipment was damaged.
3. Security and research staff should share necessary information so that section boundaries do not conceal risks.
