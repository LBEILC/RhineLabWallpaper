COORDINATES OF TOMORROW

Department: Control Section
Collection: Cross-period compilation
Related: Silence / Saria
Access: REFERENCE AREA

After major events, Rhine Lab still has to decide where its next research should lead. The discussions of scientific ethics advanced by Silence add another measure to those decisions. This volume treats research freedom, responsibility, and cooperation as continuing questions rather than presenting the future as an answer already settled.

Research notes
1. Compare technical goals with the specific lives they are intended to improve.
2. Leave room for differing positions and record where agreement has not yet been reached.
3. Continue tracking long-term effects on people and the environment after a project ends.
