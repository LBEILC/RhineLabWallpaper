以莱茵生命档案终端为灵感，将日常工具与玻璃档案阵列融入桌面的非官方《明日方舟》同人交互壁纸。

【桌面工作台】
查看时间与日期，记录最多三条今日事项，追踪重要日程，使用专注计时，显示当前歌曲、封面与播放进度。功能和界面元素可按需开启或隐藏。

【档案展示】
拖动浏览循环玻璃档案阵列，阅读 40 份档案、收藏记录或导出 TXT。进入 360° 查看器旋转、缩放和拆解模型。

【自定义画面】
支持亮暗配色与定时切换、三种音乐律动、静止呼吸、HUD 曲面与鼠标追踪，以及可调的颗粒、色散和暗角。可调整界面边距、模型精度、自定义画质和超级性能模式，也可关闭 3D，搭配自己的图片背景使用工作台。

【语言与操作】
在 WE 壁纸属性顶部选择「语言 / Language → 简体中文 / English」，即时生效并保存。原有英文开场与图形文字保持原样；你的姓名、事项、日程标题和媒体信息保留原文。
长期配置统一在 WE 属性中调整。普通选项名称跟随 WE 自身的界面语言，折叠分组标题显示中英双语。页脚可切换模式、临时打开完整工作台，也可重播开场和开关 3D；波纹接力小游戏可手动进入。页脚快捷操作在重载后恢复 WE 默认配置。
媒体信息取决于系统与播放器支持，音乐律动需要启用音频响应。语言切换保留当前档案、收藏与专注计时。

【Rhine Lab · Interactive Desktop】
An unofficial Arknights fan wallpaper inspired by the Rhine Lab archive terminal, combining everyday desktop tools with an interactive glass archive array.

【Desktop workspace】
Check the time and date, keep up to three daily tasks, track an upcoming event, run a focus timer, and view the current song, cover art and playback progress. Enable or hide individual tools and interface elements as needed.

【Archive display】
Drag through the looping glass archive array to read 40 records, save favorites or export TXT files. Open the 360° viewer to rotate, zoom and explore the model's parts.

【Appearance】
Choose light or dark themes with optional scheduled switching, three audio response styles, idle breathing, a curved HUD with mouse tracking, and adjustable grain, chromatic aberration and vignette. Adjust UI margins, model detail and graphics quality, use Super Performance Mode, or turn off 3D and pair the workspace with your own background image.

【Language and controls】
Choose Language / 语言 → 简体中文 / English at the top of the wallpaper's properties in WE. The choice applies immediately and is saved. Existing English opening graphics stay unchanged; names, tasks, event titles and media information remain as supplied.
Use WE's properties panel for persistent settings. Option labels follow WE's own interface language, while group headings show both languages. The footer lets you switch modes, temporarily open the full workspace, replay the opening and toggle 3D. You can also start the ripple relay game manually. Temporary workspace choices reset to WE defaults on reload.
Media information depends on your system and player. Audio response requires audio recording to be enabled in WE. Language changes preserve your active archive, favorites and focus timer.

Preview tasks are demonstrations; you provide your own content. All wallpaper resources run locally, with font notices and licenses included in the package. Arknights characters, names and setting belong to their respective owners. This work is not affiliated with the official creators.
